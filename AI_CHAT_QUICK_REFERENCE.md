# ChargeFlow AI Chat - Quick Reference Guide

## 🚀 Quick Start

### Access AI Chat
1. Open ChargeFlow app
2. Tap **AI** icon in bottom navigation
3. Start chatting!

---

## 💬 How to Use

### Text Messages
1. Type your question in the input field
2. Press **Send** button or **Enter** key
3. AI responds in 1-2 seconds

### Voice Messages
1. Tap **Microphone** button
2. Speak your question
3. Tap **Stop** when done
4. Preview and send
5. AI responds with voice automatically

### Listen to AI Responses
- AI automatically speaks responses
- Tap **Listen** button on any message to replay
- Tap **Stop** button in header to stop playback
- Recording new message auto-stops AI voice

---

## ⚙️ Settings

### Change Voice
1. Tap **⚙️** icon in chat header
2. Choose from 6 voices:
   - **Alloy** - Neutral, balanced
   - **Echo** - Male, clear
   - **Fable** - British accent, expressive
   - **Onyx** - Deep male, authoritative
   - **Nova** - Female, energetic
   - **Shimmer** - Soft female, natural (default)
3. Tap **Play** to test
4. Tap **Save**

### Change Quality
- **Standard** - Fast, good quality
- **HD** - Slower, more natural (default)

### Change Speed
- **0.75x** - Slow
- **1.0x** - Normal (default)
- **1.25x** - Fast
- **1.5x** - Very fast

## 📋 Booking Instructions

### Step-by-Step
1. **Open Map** - Go to map in app
2. **Select Station** - Choose station to book
3. **Choose Time** - Select date and time
4. **Pay Deposit** - 100 som deposit required
5. **Arrive On Time** - Station waits 15 minutes
6. **Start Charging** - Deposit used as payment

### Important Rules
- ⏰ **15-minute grace period** after booking start
- ❌ **Late >15 min** = Booking cancelled, deposit NOT returned
- ✅ **On time** = Deposit used for charging (kWh payment)

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### AI Not Responding
1. Check internet connection
2. Refresh the page
3. Try again in a few seconds

### Voice Not Working
1. Allow microphone permission
2. Check browser supports audio
3. Use HTTPS or localhost

### Wrong Language
1. Check your profile language setting
2. Speak clearly in your language
3. AI auto-detects from your message

### No Stations Shown
1. Enable location permission
2. Check GPS is on
3. Refresh the page

## 💡 Pro Tips

### Quick Questions
- Ask specific questions for faster answers
- Example: "CCS2 станции" instead of "Расскажи про все станции"

### Voice vs Text
- **Voice**: Faster for long questions
- **Text**: Better for specific details (addresses, prices)

### Save Time
- Use voice for general questions
- Use text for booking details
- Listen to AI responses while driving (hands-free)

### Best Practices
- Ask one question at a time
- Be specific about location
- Mention connector type if known
- Ask about availability before booking

## 📞 Common Questions

### Q: Does AI have access to my booking history?
**A:** Not yet. AI only sees current station availability.

### Q: Can AI make bookings for me?
**A:** No, AI guides you to the booking page. You complete booking there.

### Q: Is my voice data saved?
**A:** No, voice is transcribed and deleted immediately.

### Q: Can I use AI offline?
**A:** No, AI requires internet connection.

### Q: How accurate is station availability?
**A:** 100% accurate - real-time data from database.

### Q: Can AI speak English?
**A:** Yes, but app is optimized for Russian and Kyrgyz.
